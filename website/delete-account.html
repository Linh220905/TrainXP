Privacy & Control

Account & Data Deletion

We respect your right to be forgotten. You may permanently delete your TrainXP account at any time.

What happens when you delete your account:
  • Your profile and login credentials (Apple ID / Google OAuth identifier) are permanently removed from our authentication servers.
  • All workout logs, exercise history, sets, repetitions, and volume records are permanently deleted.
  • All Personal Record (PR) achievements, unlocked 3D trophies, and RPG level progression are erased.
  • Note on active subscriptions: Deleting your account does not automatically cancel an ongoing App Store subscription with Apple. Please cancel your subscription via iOS Settings prior to account deletion.

Method 1: Immediate In-App Deletion (Recommended)

If you have the TrainXP app installed on your iPhone or Android device, you can delete your account immediately with zero wait time:

  1. Open the TrainXP app.
  2. Tap the Profile tab in the bottom navigation bar.
  3. Tap the Settings (Gear icon) in the top-right corner.
  4. Scroll to the bottom of the screen and tap "Delete Account & Data".
  5. Confirm your selection in the alert prompt.

Your account and all associated database records will be erased immediately, and you will be signed out.

Method 2: Web Deletion Request

If you have uninstalled the app or cannot access your device, submit a deletion request directly to our engineering team:

Submit Deletion Request by Email

Send an email from your registered account email address to:

support@calgo.tech

Subject line: Account Deletion Request - TrainXP
Body: Please include your registered email address or Apple Relay email. We will verify your request and purge all database records within 24–48 hours, sending you a final confirmation once completed.

Questions or Inquiries?

For any privacy inquiries or assistance regarding your data rights under GDPR and CCPA, please contact our Data Protection Officer at support@calgo.tech.

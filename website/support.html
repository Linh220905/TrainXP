Frequently Asked Questions

How do I restore my TrainXP Pro purchase on a new iPhone?

If you upgraded your phone or reinstalled the app, make sure you are signed into the same Apple ID used to make the purchase. Open TrainXP → go to Profile → tap Settings (Gear icon) → select "Restore Purchases". Your Pro status will activate immediately.

How do I cancel or modify my TrainXP Pro subscription?

Subscriptions are managed securely via your Apple ID:

  1. Open the Settings app on your iPhone.
  2. Tap your Name / Apple ID banner at the very top.
  3. Tap Subscriptions.
  4. Tap TrainXP.
  5. Tap Cancel Subscription (or select a different billing tier).

You will retain full Pro access until your current billing period ends.

How do I enable Live Activities on my Lock Screen and Dynamic Island?

TrainXP supports real-time rest timers and active set loggers on iOS 16.1+:

  • Open iPhone Settings → scroll down to TrainXP.
  • Ensure Live Activities is toggled ON.
  • For fastest countdown refresh, also turn on Frequent Updates.

How do I unlock higher RPG tiers (Gold, Diamond, Prismatic)?

Trophies for Bench Press, Squat, and Deadlift are awarded based on your strength-to-bodyweight ratio:

  • Bronze / Silver: Great starting strength baseline (0.5x - 1.0x bodyweight).
  • Gold: Solid advanced lifter standard (1.0x - 1.75x bodyweight).
  • Diamond / Ruby: Elite powerlifter tier (1.25x - 2.75x bodyweight).
  • Prismatic: Legendary mastery (2.0x+ bench, 2.5x+ squat, 3.0x+ deadlift).

How do I report a bug or request a feature?

Email our product team directly at support@calgo.tech. Please include your device model, iOS version, and a brief description or screenshot of the issue.
